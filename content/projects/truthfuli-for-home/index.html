Truthfuli for Home

Truthfuli is a smart home utility product for monitoring household gas, power, and internet in real time. It reduces uncertainty around availability, cost, and service interruptions by sending proactive gas alerts, supporting refill ordering, surfacing outages, and giving providers a clearer signal before daily disruptions become surprises.
