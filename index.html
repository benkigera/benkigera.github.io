🧭 How I work

1. Start where the problem is still unclear

I do my best work in domains where the software problem is still being discovered.

2. Map the workflow first

Who makes decisions, what data they trust, where context gets lost, what is painful to repeat, and what needs to become visible.

3. Build the smallest serious version

A real data model, real UI flows, the operational edge cases, and enough structure that the prototype can keep growing.

4. Ship, then talk to users

For my own products I put the app in front of real users early, measure the funnel with product analytics, and change what the data shows is broken.
