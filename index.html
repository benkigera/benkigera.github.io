How I work

I am strongest in ambiguous domains where the software problem is still being discovered.

I start by mapping the workflow: who makes decisions, what data they trust, where context gets lost, what is painful to repeat, and what needs to become visible.

Then I build the smallest serious version: real data model, real UI flows, operational edge cases, and enough system structure that the prototype can keep evolving.
